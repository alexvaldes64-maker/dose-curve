# Code of conduct

Dose Curve is used by people managing ADHD, sleep and medication. Many contributors and users live with these too.

- Be respectful and assume good faith. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Never shame anyone for their diagnosis, their medication, or how they take it.
- Do not give medical advice to other people in issues or discussions. Point them to their prescriber or pharmacist.
- Do not post anyone's personal or health information, including your own logs, without removing identifying details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and block participants who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use GitHub's reporting tools or the private vulnerability report described in SECURITY.md.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
